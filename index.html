Privacy Policy

Sortiverse: Puzzle Worlds

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Sortiverse: Puzzle Worlds (“Sortiverse,” “we,” or “the App”) is an iOS puzzle game published by an independent developer. This policy describes how information is handled when you play.

1. Information you provide and local game data

You do not need an account or need to provide your name or email address to play. The App saves gameplay information, such as level progress, preferences, and settings, on your device. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send so we can respond.

2. Advertising and third-party data

Sortiverse uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process device and app information, advertising identifiers where permitted, ad interactions, approximate location inferred from technical information, and diagnostic or fraud-prevention information. This processing may support ad delivery, measurement, personalization where permitted, and security. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's partner-services explanation.

3. Consent and your choices

Where required, the App may ask for advertising consent or provide privacy choices. Available choices may vary by region. You can also manage app tracking permissions in your device's iOS settings. If the App offers a “Privacy choices” option, you can use it to revisit applicable consent choices.

4. Children's privacy

The App is designed for a general audience and does not require children to submit personal information to play. Advertising data processing is subject to applicable laws and the settings configured for the advertising service. If you have a concern about a child's information, please contact us.

5. Sharing and retention

Information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners as described above. Local gameplay data generally remains on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Google and its partners apply their own retention practices to data they process. Emails sent to us may be retained as needed to address your request.

6. Security

We seek to limit the personal information handled directly by the App. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the App, its services, or legal requirements change. We will post an updated effective date here.
