Sortiverse Support

Sortiverse: Puzzle Worlds is a sorting puzzle game for iPhone.

Contact support

Need help with a puzzle, found a bug, or have a suggestion? Use the form below to send a support request. Please avoid including passwords or sensitive personal information.

If the embedded form does not load, open the support form in a new tab.

Quick troubleshooting

  • If the game freezes or behaves unexpectedly, close and reopen Sortiverse.
  • If a problem continues, restart your iPhone and check for an app update in the App Store.
  • If a specific puzzle seems stuck, contact support with the level number and theme.

Note: Deleting the app may erase locally stored game progress. Contact support before reinstalling if you want to preserve your progress.
